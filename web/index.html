Add a companion

Get ready-made characters from the page-mascot gallery (53 animals, people and robots, in several styles).

  1. Hover a character, press Use, and download both <name>-directions.webp and <name>-reactions.webp.
  2. Choose both files below (together in either box works), then press Add companion. The name is filled in from the file name.

Your own artwork works too: two square sheets of 3×3 cells on a transparent background, like cat-directions and cat-reactions. The pet follows your finger and reacts to taps. Cells, left to right and top to bottom:

    A GIF, animated WebP or APNG (a still PNG or JPEG works too). It is fitted into 64×64 pixels and loops on the pet page; up to 2000 frames and 120 seconds.

    Alarm ringtone

    Converted to 16 kHz mono, at most 30 seconds, and looped while the alarm rings. A custom ringtone needs firmware 1.9.0 or newer.

    Save and flash

    Flashing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer, with the board plugged in over USB. To flash the downloaded file yourself, write it at 0x610000.
