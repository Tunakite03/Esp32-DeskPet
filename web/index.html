Make the desk pet yours

Pick the companions and the alarm ringtone, then flash them straight to the board. Only the asset partition is written: Wi-Fi, alarms and other settings stay as they are.

Companions

The order here is the order in the picker on the board. Hover a picture to play it.

Add a companion

Tap characters to pick them.

Characters by Kamran Ahmed from page-mascot (MIT license). Each follows your finger on the board and reacts to taps.

Two 3×3 sheets on a transparent background, read left to right, top to bottom. Sheets from page-mascot work as they are (example: directions, reactions).

Tip: pick both files at once; the name comes from <name>-directions.webp.

A GIF, animated WebP or APNG (a still PNG or JPEG works too). It is fitted into 64×64 pixels and loops on the pet page; up to 2000 frames and 120 seconds.

Alarm ringtone

Converted to 16 kHz mono, at most 30 seconds, and looped while the alarm rings. A custom ringtone needs firmware 1.9.0 or newer.

Save and flash

Flashing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer, with the board plugged in over USB. To flash the downloaded file yourself, write it at 0x610000.
